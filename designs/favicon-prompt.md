# Favicon

The silver great helm and golden sound waves identify the medieval voice
soundboard without favouring either faction. Colours match the navy and gold
interface. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, with transparency.

Source artwork: `designs/favicon.png`.
Browser exports: `website/favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 pixels) and
`website/soundboard/assets/favicon.png` (64 pixels). Both preserve transparency
and use Lanczos downsampling from the source artwork. The three HTML pages and
the full-list generation template reference the same files.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-concept. Asset type: production favicon for Chivalry: Medieval Warfare fan voice-command soundboard. Create one square icon: a bold silver medieval great helm, front-facing, with a clear black T-shaped visor opening, paired with two thick golden curved sound-wave marks immediately to its right. Both sit inside a single midnight-navy (#0b1b30) medieval heater shield with a restrained thick antique-gold (#dfbd6d) rim. The helmet occupies most of the shield and is immediately recognizable. Palette silver/ivory, antique gold, midnight navy, tiny steel-blue accents. Style: crisp premium medieval game GUI insignia, restrained shallow metallic shading, flat clean silhouette; very simplified large forms readable at 16x16 and 32x32 pixels. Avoid small holes, rivets, flourishes, busy texture, lettering, drop shadows outside shield, photorealism, background scenery, mockups, or multiple icon variants. Center the single shield, occupying 92% of square canvas in each dimension. Fully transparent background outside shield. No text. This is the finished icon artwork, not a presentation sheet.
